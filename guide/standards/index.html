REYO / COMPANY STANDARDS

Make your engineering guide enforceable.

Reyo checks the rules your company wrote down and identifies the clause a finding breaks.

01

Exact requirements

Requirements are extracted word for word from your document, without paraphrasing.

02

Locked versions

Approved versions are locked. Previous scans retain the standard as it was at the time of review.

03

Governed exceptions

A waiver requires an approver and an expiry date. It appears in every report.

04

Scope and compliance mapping

Scope requirements to an organisation, team, repository or folder. Map them to SOC 2 and ISO 27001. These are control mappings, not a claim that using Reyo makes an organisation certified.
