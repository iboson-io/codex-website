Product guide Overview
REYO / OVERVIEW

Code quality, on your terms.

Reyo by iBoson reviews repositories and pull requests with a self-learning rule engine and the standards your team wrote.

01

The core utility

Bring static analysis, security, dependencies, infrastructure checks and optional AI review into one report, on a shared severity scale. Findings show the code, the rule and the company clause behind them.

02

Who it is for

Individual developers work locally in VS Code or the CLI. Engineering teams review pull requests. DevOps teams apply CI gates and deploy on Kubernetes. Organisations use standards, approvals and audit history to govern engineering work.

03

Early access

Reyo is in early access. Contact the iBoson team for access and installation instructions. This guide organises the capabilities published on the source product site; it is not an SDK or API reference.
