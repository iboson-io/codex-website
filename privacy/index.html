REYO / PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy policy.

How iBoson collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use Reyo. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell it.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

01

Who we are

Reyo is a product of iBoson Innovations Private Limited (“iBoson”, “we”, “us”). This policy covers the reyo.dev website, the hosted Reyo service, the Reyo VS Code extension and the command-line tools (together, the “Service”).

02

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the Service:

  • Account details — your name, email address, organisation and role. If you sign in with GitHub, the basic profile GitHub shares with us. Passwords are never stored, only a salted hash of them.
  • Repository data — the files of the branches you connect, and the metadata Reyo needs for analysis and engineering metrics, such as commits, authors, pull requests and reviewers. Files that match secret-bearing or binary patterns are not read, so their contents are never stored and never sent to an AI provider.
  • Billing details — Paddle, our merchant of record, collects your payment details. We receive only your plan, seat count, invoices and subscription status. We never see or store your card number.
  • Technical and usage data — IP address, browser, device, access times and service logs, used to keep the Service secure and working.
  • Communications — what you send us when you contact support or ask for early access.
03

Local analysis

The Reyo VS Code extension analyses your code on your own machine. Your code does not leave your computer unless you turn on the extension’s AI setting, in which case the relevant code is sent to the AI provider you chose.

04

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • provide, operate and maintain the Service, and authenticate you;
  • analyse the repositories you connect and show you the results;
  • send service messages such as address verification, password resets, invitations and billing notices;
  • provide technical support and administer your subscription;
  • keep the Service secure, and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • improve the Service; and
  • meet our legal obligations.
05

AI providers

AI review is optional and off by default. When your organisation turns it on, Reyo sends small context slices — a route with its middleware, a function with the query it runs — not the whole repository, to the AI provider your organisation configures (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI or Mistral), using your organisation’s own key. That provider processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy.

06

How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us run the Service — hosting, email delivery and payments (Paddle) — under obligations to protect it;
  • with the AI provider and the Git providers (such as GitHub) that you or your organisation choose to connect;
  • with the other members of your organisation, as the Service’s workspace features require; and
  • when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of iBoson, our users or others.
07

Cookies and browser storage

The Service uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to protect sign-in flows. They are required for the Service to work.

This website uses no analytics or advertising cookies. It stores one setting in your browser — whether you paused animations — and that setting never leaves your device.

08

Retention and deletion

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. Organisation administrators can set how long author attribution is kept.

You can delete your account at any time. It enters a 30-day grace period, during which you can cancel the deletion; after that your account data is removed. We may keep limited records for longer where the law requires it, such as invoices.

09

Security

Passwords and session tokens are stored only as hashes, and the access tokens and secrets the Service holds are encrypted. Access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you of a breach where the law requires it.

10

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it, by writing to info@iboson.io. We will respond within a reasonable time and as the law requires.

11

Children

The Service is meant for professionals and is not directed at anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new date, and tell you about material changes through the Service or by email.

13

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: info@iboson.io.
